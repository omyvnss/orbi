Changelog

What's new in Orbi.

v0.2 — fits your notch, knows your sessions

  • Fits the real notch. Orbi measures your MacBook's camera housing and hangs just below it, so its face is never hidden behind the camera. Small curved corners let it flow out of the screen edge.
  • Tucks away when idle. When no agent is doing anything, Orbi slips back into the notch. Hover the top of the screen to peek.
  • Appearance settings. Face size, width and height, and where it sits: drag it anywhere, or snap it back into the notch.
  • Sessions. Every running Claude Code session shows up by project name, with a small dot per session on the face and a line for sub-agents.
  • Questions. When Claude asks you a multiple-choice question, Orbi shows it and its options. ⌃⌥J takes you to the exact terminal tab to answer.
  • ⌃⌥J jumps to the exact tab in Terminal and iTerm, not just the app. macOS asks once for permission.
  • See before you connect. Connect now shows the exact lines it will add to the agent's config first. Values that look like keys are hidden.
  • A soft chime when an agent needs you (Settings → Appearance; off with one switch), and a new palette: cream, gold, sage and clay.

v0.1 — first release

  • A face in your Mac's notch with five moods: idle, working, asking, done and denied.
  • Approve or deny with ⌃⌥A / ⌃⌥D from any app; ⌃⌥O shows details, ⌃⌥J jumps to the agent's terminal.
  • One-click connect for Claude Code, Codex and OpenCode (approvals), plus Gemini CLI, Cursor and Hermes (status).
  • Plain-English, one-line explanations with warnings for risky commands.
  • Optional model-written explanations with your own key (Ollama, OpenRouter, OpenAI).
  • Local only, with a signed handshake and a 45-second fallback to your terminal.
