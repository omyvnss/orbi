Privacy

Orbi collects nothing. There are no accounts, no analytics and no cloud.

What stays on your Mac

  • The commands, file paths and requests your agents send to Orbi are shown on your screen and then forgotten. They are never stored or uploaded.
  • Orbi's settings, token and port live in ~/Library/Application Support/Orbi.
  • Explanations are written by built-in rules, on your Mac, instantly.

Optional: your own model

If you turn on model explanations in Settings, the request (for example the command an agent wants to run) is sent to the provider you choose — such as Ollama on your own Mac, or OpenRouter. Orbi shows a notice before you enable a cloud provider. API keys are kept in the macOS Keychain.
